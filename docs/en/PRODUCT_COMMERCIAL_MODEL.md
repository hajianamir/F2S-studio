**Fire Protection Design & Simulation Platform**

## 1. Commercial Pricing Tiers, Market Justification & Free Trial
- **14-Day Free Trial Tier ($0 / 14 Days — R-96 Resolved)**: Includes 1 active project, 1 GB storage, full C++ hydraulic calculations, and WebGL viewer. *Digital PE seal signing is disabled on trial*.
- **Professional Engineer (PE) Plan ($499 / month per seat — R-96 Resolved)**:
  - *Market Comparison*: $499/mo replaces $800–$1,200/mo in fragmented single-purpose desktop licenses (HydraCALC + SprinkCAD + Revit MEP plugins + report tools), delivering an integrated workflow saving 15+ hours/project.
- **Enterprise Firm Plan ($2,499 / month per firm — R-96 Resolved)**: Includes up to 10 seats ($199/mo per additional seat), dedicated schema isolation, custom domain, and priority support.
- **Beta Firm Transition Terms (R-97 Resolved)**: Beta Firms A & B receive a perpetual **50% commercial discount** ($1,249/mo Enterprise Plan) as consideration for early validation.
- **AHJ / Municipal License ($0 Freemium)**: Free verification licenses for municipal plan review officers.

## 3. SLA Adjustment, Remedies & Point-of-Signing Legal Modal

### Stage 1 SLA Adjustment & Service Credits (R-95 Resolved)
- **Stage 1 SLA Target**: **99.5% Uptime** for core API endpoints (~3.6h allowable downtime/month).
- **SLA Remedy Credits**:
  - Availability 99.0% – 99.49%: **10% monthly subscription credit**.
  - Availability < 99.0%: **25% monthly subscription credit**.
- **Stage 2 Transition**: SLA upgrades to 99.9% upon activation of Multi-AZ infrastructure.

### Point-of-Signing Legal Disclaimer Modal (R-102 Resolved)
- When a PE clicks *"Sign and Seal"*, a mandatory confirmation modal appears:
  > **Legal PE Responsibility Confirmation**:
  > *"By affixing your digital seal, you confirm that you have independently reviewed and verified all calculation inputs, hydraulic network configurations, and output reports, accepting 100% professional liability under your engineering license."*

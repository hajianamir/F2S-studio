# Terms of Service, Master Services Agreement (MSA) & Data Processing Addendum (DPA)
**Fire Protection Design & Simulation Platform**

---

## 1. Terms of Service & Professional Engineering Disclaimer
- **Platform Function**: The software serves strictly as a computational, drafting, and compliance verification assistant.
- **Licensure & Seal Authority**: The platform does NOT practice engineering. The licensed Professional Engineer (PE) of Record who affixes their digital seal retains **100% legal and professional liability** for all design decisions, calculations, and municipal submittals.
- **Point-of-Signing Modal**: Every digital seal execution surfaces a mandatory modal:
  > *"By affixing your seal, you confirm that you have independently reviewed and verified all calculation inputs, hydraulic network configurations, and output reports, accepting full professional liability under your engineering license."*

---

## 2. Master Services Agreement (MSA) & SLA Credits
- **Stage 1 Availability SLA**: **99.5% Uptime** for core API endpoints.
- **SLA Remedy Schedule**:
  - Availability 99.0% – 99.49%: **10% monthly subscription credit**.
  - Availability < 99.0%: **25% monthly subscription credit**.
- **Data Ownership**: Customer retains 100% ownership of all BIM IFC4 models, network graphs, and calculation artifacts.

---

## 3. Data Processing Addendum (DPA) & AEC Confidentiality
- **Data Isolation**: Strict Row-Level Security (RLS) and schema-per-tenant isolation prevent cross-tenant data access.
- **Data Retention & Deletion**: Upon contract termination, customer data is exportable for 30 days and purged within 60 days, excluding immutable audit log hashes required by law.

## 1. Phase 6 Technical Objectives & Deferred Scope
Phase 6 focuses on expanding technical ruleset coverage, enterprise multi-region cloud infrastructure, and advanced 3D WebGPU visualization performance.

- **Sprint 6.1**: General High-Piled Storage Rules Expansion (Group A/B/C Plastics, Roll Paper, Rubber Tires).
- **Sprint 6.2**: In-Rack Sprinkler Matrix Calculations (NFPA 13 Chapter 25).
- **Sprint 6.3**: Stage 2 Enterprise Cloud Infrastructure (AWS EKS Multi-AZ, Dedicated CloudHSM, 7-Year WORM Storage).
- **Sprint 6.4**: WebGPU Full-Model View Optimization (30+ FPS at 1M mixed primitives in full overview mode).
- **Sprint 6.5**: Multi-Constraint AI Layout Optimization Extensions (Multi-Standard NFPA/EN/BS optimization).
- **Sprint 6.6**: Enterprise SSO Integration (SAML 2.0 / OIDC / Azure AD / Okta).
